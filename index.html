HOW TO PLAY

  • 🏃 Move: ←→ or AD · Jump: SPACE / W (hold = higher)
  • 📱 Touch: on-screen ◀ ▶ and ⤒ jump button.
  • 🎯 Goal: reach the glowing EXIT PORTAL. Collect ◈ Energy Orbs for stars.
  • ⚠ Avoid spikes & pits. Checkpoints save your respawn.
  • 🔘 Pressure plates open doors while held — push crates onto them. Touch switches toggle gates.
  • 🌀 Gravity pads flip your gravity — walk on ceilings! Launch pads send you flying.
  • ⭐ Stars: finish = 1 · all orbs = +1 · no deaths = +1. R restart · ESC pause.

ABOUT

ALPHA GRAVITY is an original 30-level 2D physics puzzle platformer. Guide the Alpha Runner through neon sectors: push crates, hold switches, ride movers, bounce on launch pads and flip gravity itself.

Built with pure HTML5 Canvas + WebAudio — no engines, no assets. Everything is procedural: art, music, sound.
